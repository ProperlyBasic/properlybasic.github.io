Kingdom Paradox is a podcast by two Christian men who want to create the analysis and guidance we wish we had when we were younger. 

We discuss theology, philosophy, history, current topics, and many other things, all with a focus on scripture and the paradox of being a part of the city of God while living on this earth. 

This site features some stand alone articles and a searchable list podcast episodes. Use the buttons on the bottom-left to subscribe the the podcast on platforms like Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Youtube, and more.
